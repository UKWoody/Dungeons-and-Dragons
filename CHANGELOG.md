# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (Initial Release)

### Added
- The Black Oracle (Artifact)
  - Eyes in the Dark (darkvision)
  - Oracle's Guidance (skill advantage on choice)
  - Whispered Warning (surprise prevention)
  
- The Crimson Visage (Artifact)
  - Eyes of the Night (darkvision)
  - Predatory Instinct (skill advantage on choice)
  - Blood Will Tell (HP preservation)
  - Noble Bearing (Persuasion proficiency/expertise)
  
- The Infernal Choker of Bel (Artifact, Warlock attunement)
  - Bel's Protection (Psychic resistance)
  - Commanding Presence (Persuasion proficiency/expertise)
  - Strategic Insight (ability check bonus)
  - Infernal Audience (once per long rest question)

### Features
- Full active effects implementation
- Personality lines and flavor text
- Attunement requirements
- Resource tracking for limited abilities
